# ANKA Code of Conduct

## 1. Our Commitment

ANKA is an independent technology initiative dedicated to artificial intelligence, robotics, software engineering, and innovation.

We aim to maintain a respectful, inclusive, professional, and collaborative environment for all contributors.

## 2. Expected Behavior

All ANKA members and contributors are expected to:

- Treat others with respect and professionalism.
- Communicate clearly and constructively.
- Respect different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Protect confidential information.
- Respect intellectual property and software licenses.
- Support teamwork and responsible innovation.
- Follow applicable laws and GitHub policies.

## 3. Unacceptable Behavior

The following actions are not permitted:

- Harassment, threats, bullying, or discrimination.
- Personal attacks or abusive language.
- Unauthorized disclosure of private information.
- Misuse of organizational resources.
- Deliberate disruption of projects.
- Sharing malicious software or stolen credentials.
- Unauthorized access to repositories or accounts.

## 4. Team Collaboration

ANKA consists of six organizational teams:

1. Software Engineering
2. Hardware Engineering
3. Customer Support
4. Artificial Intelligence & Robotics
5. Research & Development
6. Cybersecurity & Infrastructure

Each team should communicate respectfully, document important technical decisions, and follow applicable project guidelines.

## 5. Reporting Violations

To report inappropriate behavior, contact:

**support.anka@proton.me**

Reports will be reviewed according to the available information and organizational resources.

## 6. Enforcement

Depending on the circumstances, maintainers may:

- Issue a warning.
- Remove inappropriate content.
- Restrict repository participation.
- Revoke organizational access.

Decisions should be fair, proportionate, and based on available evidence.

## 7. Appeals

Individuals affected by enforcement decisions may request a review through the official contact email.

## 8. Scope

This policy applies to ANKA's official GitHub repositories, discussions, issues, pull requests, and other designated collaboration spaces.
