# ANKA Support Center

**Building Intelligent Technologies**

Welcome to the official support information page of ANKA.

ANKA is an independent technology initiative focused on artificial intelligence, robotics, software engineering, hardware development, and technology research.

## 1. Contact Information

For general questions, technical assistance, or collaboration inquiries, contact:

**Official Email:** support.anka@proton.me

**GitHub:** https://github.com/anka-organization

Support availability may vary depending on available resources.

## 2. Supported Project Areas

### ChatPCT — Artificial Intelligence

Questions concerning the planned ChatPCT AI platform, software architecture, and development activities.

### Vector Robotics

Research involving robot software, SDK integration, independent robot infrastructure, and AI-assisted interaction.

### ANKA Web

Web development, interface design, and digital platform projects.

### Research & Development

Questions about experimental technology projects, technical research, and potential collaboration.

## 3. Reporting Technical Problems

When reporting an issue, please provide:

1. Project or repository name.
2. A clear description of the problem.
3. Steps required to reproduce the issue.
4. Expected behavior.
5. Actual behavior.
6. Relevant error messages.
7. Operating system and software version, if applicable.

Please never share passwords, private keys, API tokens, or sensitive personal information.

## 4. GitHub Issues

Where GitHub Issues are enabled, users may submit non-sensitive bug reports and feature suggestions.

Before opening a new issue, please check whether a similar report already exists.

## 5. Security Vulnerabilities

Security vulnerabilities must not be disclosed through public issues.

Please follow ANKA's SECURITY.md policy and report concerns privately.

## 6. Customer Support Team

ANKA has established a Customer Support team within its GitHub organizational structure.

The team is intended to coordinate user communication and technical assistance as these activities develop.

Creating this team does not imply guaranteed staffing or continuous support availability.

## 7. Response Times

ANKA currently operates as an independent technology initiative.

We do not guarantee specific response times, continuous availability, or service-level agreements.

## 8. Collaboration Requests

Organizations, developers, researchers, and technology teams interested in collaboration may contact:

support.anka@proton.me

Please include a short introduction, the proposed area of collaboration, and relevant technical information.

## 9. Privacy and Confidentiality

Do not share confidential business information, personal data, passwords, or sensitive technical details in public GitHub issues.

Private information should only be shared through appropriate and trusted communication channels.

## 10. About ANKA

ANKA is an independent technology initiative and is not currently a registered company.

Its project portfolio includes planned, experimental, and developing technologies.
