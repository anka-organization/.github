# ANKA 🚀

### Building Intelligent Technologies

**Artificial Intelligence | Robotics | Software Engineering | Research**

Welcome to **ANKA**, an independent technology initiative dedicated to researching, designing, and developing innovative technologies.

## 🌐 About ANKA

ANKA focuses on artificial intelligence, robotics, intelligent software, natural language processing, and human–robot interaction.

Our mission is to explore practical technological solutions, develop experimental prototypes, and encourage technical collaboration.

## 🔬 Research & Development

- **Artificial Intelligence:** AI assistants, language models, and intelligent systems.
- **Robotics:** Robot software, control interfaces, and autonomy research.
- **Natural Language Processing:** Turkish voice interfaces and intelligent interaction.
- **Software Engineering:** Modern applications, automation, and web technologies.
- **Open Technologies:** Research involving open-source tools and developer ecosystems.

## 💻 Our Initiatives

### ChatPCT
An AI assistant platform in planning and development, with proposed agent capabilities, project workspaces, memory systems, and developer tools.

### Vector Robotics Research
Exploring Anki Vector SDK connectivity, independent software infrastructure, voice interaction, and AI-assisted robot control.

### ANKA Web
Web development experiments, digital platforms, and interactive applications.

## 🤝 Collaboration

ANKA welcomes technical discussions, research exchanges, and opportunities to develop practical prototypes with other technology teams and organizations.
